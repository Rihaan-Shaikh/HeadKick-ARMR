HOW TO PLAY TILT KICK

STEP 01

TILT TO AIM

Tilt your head left or right to move the aim reticle across the goal mouth. Your head roll steers horizontal placement.

STEP 02

HOLD TO CHARGE

Remain tilted to charge shot power from 0% to 100%. Aim stays perfectly stable while charging.

STEP 03

NOD UP TO KICK

Execute a quick UPWARD nod with your head to fire the penalty! You never need to return your head to neutral center.

STEP 01

STAND IN FRAME

Step back so your upper body and legs are clearly visible to the webcam.

STEP 02

LEAN TO AIM

Lean your torso left or right to direct the shot into the corners. Hold stance to build power.

STEP 03

KICK OR SNAP

Lift your kicking foot or snap back to neutral center to release the shot!

ACCESSIBILITY / KEYBOARD CONTROLS

No webcam? Use ← / → or A / D to aim, hold SPACEBAR to charge power, press ↑ (or release Space) to kick, R to reset, and ESC for pause.
